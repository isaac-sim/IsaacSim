## [7.0.0a1] - 2026-09-16

### Added

- The Falling Cube sequence demonstrates Foundation stage authoring, OvPhysX simulation, and OVGL visualization.
- The Falling Cube OV SIM sequence compares in-process and gRPC clients for the same authoring and simulation workflow.
- The Motion Generation collection demonstrates mobile-base control, Cartesian target tracking, joint-trajectory
  following, and state-machine-driven pick and place.
- The Physics Sensors collection derives IMU and joint-state readings from simulated physics state.
- The OVGL Viewport example combines Foundation stage authoring with the lightweight C++ debug viewport.
